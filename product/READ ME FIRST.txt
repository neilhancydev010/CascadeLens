GAIA CascadeLens
Following change on Earth's surface, one radar observation at a time.

TO START
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere with a short path, such as C:\CascadeLens.
  2. Double-click "Start CascadeLens.bat".
  3. The app opens in your default browser. Keep the black window open while you
     use it.

To use Brave or another browser, paste the address from the black window into
it. It is usually http://localhost:8765

Do NOT open app\index.html directly. Browsers block a page opened that way from
loading its scripts and data, so it cannot start.

On macOS or Linux, open a terminal in this folder and run:  sh start.sh

On Windows it needs nothing installed: it uses Python 3 when you have it, and
Windows' own PowerShell when you do not. On macOS or Linux it needs Python 3.
It needs no internet connection and no login.


WHAT IS INSIDE

  Rasuwa 2026 (NISAR)
    The main case: real NISAR radar measurements of the 26 August 2026 disaster
    in Nepal, with independent UNOSAT, Sentinel-1 and OpenStreetMap layers.

  Tsho Rolpa glacial lake 2026 (NISAR)
    A second case, chosen from the Case study menu at the top left: the lake's
    size on four NISAR radar dates through the 2026 monsoon, with a labelled
    what-if dam-break scenario. It does not measure movement of the dam or the
    glacier.


HOW TO USE IT
  - Pick a place under "Jump to", or click the map.
  - Open the Inspector tab to read the stored value at that point.
  - Open the Interpretation tab to see what a layer means and what it cannot show.
  - Start the guided story for a walk through the evidence of each case.
  - Open "What is real?" for an honest status of every capability.
  - On a phone, the Layers button opens the controls, the case menu and the
    status list.


IMPORTANT LIMITS
  CascadeLens is a retrospective evidence explorer. It is not a warning system.
  NISAR products here are Provisional. No displacement is calculated.
  Radar observability was rebuilt on 13 November 2026 with the product's
  own quality mask; figures from before that date are withdrawn.
  Low coherence is evidence to examine, not proof of what happened.
  Details are in source\docs\LIMITATIONS.md.
