GAIA CascadeLens
Following Earth's movement, one radar observation at a time.

TO START
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere with a short path, such as C:\CascadeLens.
  2. Double-click "Start CascadeLens.bat".
  3. The app opens in your default browser. Keep the black window open while you
     use it.

To use Brave or another browser, paste the address from the black window into
it. It is usually http://localhost:8765

Do NOT open app\index.html directly. Browsers block a page opened that way from
loading its scripts and data, so it cannot start.

On macOS or Linux, run:  python3 serve.py

It needs Python 3, and nothing else. It needs no internet connection, no login,
and no installing.


WHAT IS INSIDE

  Rasuwa 2026 (NISAR)
    The main case: real NISAR radar measurements of the 26 August 2026 disaster
    in Nepal, with independent UNOSAT, Sentinel-1 and OpenStreetMap layers.

  LANDMET test, 31 Dec 2007
    A test case, chosen from the Case study menu at the top left. It is global
    land weather data from 2007. It is NOT NISAR data, and it says nothing about
    Rasuwa. It shows that the product can load a completely different dataset.


HOW TO USE IT
  - Pick a place under "Go to", or click the map.
  - Open the Inspector tab to read the stored value at that point.
  - Open the Interpretation tab to see what a layer means and what it cannot show.
  - Start the guided story for a walk through the Rasuwa evidence.


IMPORTANT LIMITS
  CascadeLens is a retrospective evidence explorer. It is not a warning system.
  NISAR products here are Provisional. No displacement is calculated.
  Low coherence is evidence to examine, not proof of what happened.
  Details are in source\docs\LIMITATIONS.md.
